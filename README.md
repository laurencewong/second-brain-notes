These are public notes from my personal "second brain". I'm a product manager, and I keep a private notes system for whatever I'm learning: videos I've broken down, profiles of people whose thinking I return to, and short write-ups of single ideas. This repo holds the parts that stand on their own without the personal context around them.

I wrote the notes with Claude's help, working from my own notes and the public sources behind them (videos, books, essays, interviews, public filings). Everything is restated in plain language. Direct quotes are short and attributed, and none of the notes is a transcript or a copy of someone else's writing.

## What's here

```
notes/
  youtube/   video and podcast breakdowns (AI agents, product, startups, business)
  thinkers/  "Key ideas from <name>": investors, product leaders, marketers, scientists
  people/    profiles of founders and historical figures
  lectures/  short notes on a single idea
  books/     longer notes on one book
  atomic/    small single-topic reference notes
```

## How a note is structured

- **The source comes first.** Video notes open with the title, channel and link. Thinker and people notes close with a Sources list of books, essays, talks and filings.
- **The ideas come next,** reorganized by theme rather than in the order they were said.
- **Video notes end with takeaways:** a few points you can act on.

Thinker notes summarize what that person has said and written publicly. They aren't role-play, and they don't speak for the person. Where a claim is my inference rather than something the person said, the note says so.

## Caveats

- These are notes, not advice.
- Figures quoted from videos (revenue, user counts, prices) are as the speaker stated them. They weren't independently verified unless the note says otherwise.
- Every note links its source. Go to the original before relying on any of it.

`QUEUE.md` lists the order these notes will be released in. `AUDIT.md` records what was left out and why.

## How this repo grows
One note is added each weekday, in a curated order. The history shows when each one went up.

## Built with Claude, and how
Claude drafted these from my private notes and the public sources. I chose what to keep, cut anything personal, and decide what gets published and when.
